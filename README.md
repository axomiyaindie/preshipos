## Why PreShipOS?

Building a product has never been more accessible. But knowing what to build, what to do next, and how to get a product in front of the right people is still challenging.

Independent makers often jump between scattered tutorials, tools, checklists, communities, and resources. They spend time searching for answers instead of making progress.

PreShipOS was created to bring that fragmented journey into one open, structured ecosystem.

Our goal is simple:

**Help anyone go from an idea to a shipped product with less guesswork and more confidence.**

We believe useful product-building knowledge should be accessible, practical, and continuously improved by the people who use it.

## What is PreShipOS?

PreShipOS is an open-source product-building ecosystem designed around the complete journey of an indie maker.

It brings together:

- **Knowledge:** Practical documentation and guides for every stage of building a product.
- **Resources:** Useful platforms, tools, services, and references.
- **Workflows:** Repeatable processes, checklists, and frameworks.
- **Tools:** Experiences that help makers plan, evaluate, and improve their products.
- **Community:** An opportunity for builders to share knowledge and improve the ecosystem together.

The repository is the open foundation of this ecosystem. Its documentation is available directly on GitHub, while the web application provides a dedicated experience for exploring and using the knowledge.

## The journey from idea to growth

PreShipOS organizes product building into eight connected stages.

| Stage | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **01 — Validate** | Research problems, understand users, and evaluate ideas. |
| **02 — Build** | Plan, architect, and develop products. |
| **03 — Check** | Review quality, usability, security, and readiness. |
| **04 — Prepare** | Organize branding, documentation, infrastructure, and launch essentials. |
| **05 — Launch** | Release products and reach the first users. |
| **06 — Distribute** | Get products in front of relevant audiences and platforms. |
| **07 — Discover** | Improve discoverability through search, directories, and communities. |
| **08 — Grow** | Learn from users, improve products, and build sustainable growth. |

These stages provide direction, not restrictions. Makers can explore whichever stage is relevant to their current needs.

## Who is it for?

PreShipOS is built for people who want to create and ship products.

- **Indie makers:** Find direction and turn ideas into actionable plans.
- **Solo founders:** Navigate the product journey independently.
- **Developers:** Understand product strategy beyond writing code.
- **First-time builders:** Learn how to take a product from concept to launch.
- **Students:** Explore practical product development and entrepreneurship.
- **Small teams:** Share knowledge, workflows, and repeatable processes.
- **Open-source contributors:** Help build a useful resource for the wider maker community.

You don't need to be an experienced entrepreneur. You just need an idea and the willingness to make progress.
